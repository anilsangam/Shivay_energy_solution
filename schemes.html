MINISTRY OF NEW & RENEWABLE ENERGY (MNRE)

Power Your Home For Free With Solar Subsidy

Under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, the Central Government provides financial assistance (subsidy) directly into the bank accounts of residential households to install rooftop solar panels.

As an authorized solar installation partner in Raisen and Madhya Pradesh, SHIVAY ENERGY SOLUTIONS handles the entire process end-to-end: DISCOM registration on the national portal, site feasibility approval, supply of DCR compliant panels, bi-directional net-meter installation, and final subsidy disbursement.

Who is Eligible?

  • Must be an Indian citizen with a valid residential electricity connection (DISCOM / MPPKVVCL).
  • Must have shadow-free rooftop space owned by the applicant (approx. 100 sq ft per kW).
  • Solar modules and inverters must be DCR compliant (Domestic Content Requirement) from approved manufacturers.
  • Subsidy is credited directly to customer's Aadhaar-linked bank account through DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer).

Required Documents

  • Latest Electricity Bill (with consumer number / IVRS)
  • Aadhaar Card of the electricity consumer
  • Rooftop ownership proof / property tax receipt
  • Bank Account Passbook / Cancelled Cheque (Aadhaar linked)
  • Passport size photograph

5 Easy Steps to Get Your Subsidy

1
Registration

Register on pmsuryaghar.gov.in selecting MP State and your DISCOM (e.g. MPMKVVCL / MPPKVVCL).

2
Feasibility Approval

Submit application and obtain technical feasibility approval from your DISCOM.

3
Installation by Shivay Energy

4
Net Meter & Inspection

DISCOM inspection and bidirectional net meter installation.

5
Direct Subsidy Credit

Submit commissioning report and receive up to ₹78,000 subsidy directly in your bank account.
