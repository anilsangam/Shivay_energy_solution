About Us

Solar Solutions for Maximum Energy Savings

At SHIVAY ENERGY SOLUTIONS, we are passionate about harnessing the power of the sun to create a cleaner, more sustainable future. Since our founding in Raisen, Madhya Pradesh, we've been committed to making solar energy accessible and affordable for homeowners and businesses alike. Our team of experts specializes in designing and installing high-efficiency solar systems tailored to each customer's unique energy needs, helping them reduce electricity bills while lowering their carbon footprint.

We believe that switching to solar shouldn't be complicated. That's why we offer end-to-end solutions, from consultation and financing to installation and ongoing maintenance. As an authorized partner, we help families avail up to ₹78,000 direct bank subsidy under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana. By choosing SHIVAY ENERGY SOLUTIONS, you're not just saving money—you're investing in a greener tomorrow.
